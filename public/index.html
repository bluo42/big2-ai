House rules

  • Order: ranks 3 < 4 … K < A < 2; suits ♦ < ♣ < ♥ < ♠. Lowest dealt card opens and must be played.
  • Plays: singles, pairs, and 5-card poker hands — no lone triples. Straights by top card then its suit; flushes by suit first; full houses by the triple.
  • Passing: soft pass — passing only skips your turn; you may still play when the trick comes back around. A full round of passes ends the trick.
  • Payout: losers pay the winner their cards remaining; 10–12 cards pay double, all 13 pay triple.
